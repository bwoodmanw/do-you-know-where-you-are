(Brent, 21 Nov), not free.

## Level 10 and level 20 picks

| Girl | Level 10 | Level 20 |
|---|---|---|
| Juliette | **Rolling Shell**: she creeps slowly while tucked. | **Shell Toss**: when Shell Up ends, R again within 3 s throws her shell where she aims. The host is dizzy for 2 s if it hits, and the shell bounces back to her. Recharge 45 s on a throw. |
| Gillian | **Long Juke**: the dash goes farther. | **Spin Move**: a juke straight through the host leaves him dizzy for 1 s. |
| Vivienne | **Big Cheer**: Cheer! reaches farther and gives everyone in it half their stamina back. | **Pep Talk**: Cheer! also gives a short speed burst. |
| Charlotte | **Sharp Eyes**: Lookout lasts longer. | **Early Warning** (a second passive, always on): when the host comes within 5 tiles, Charlotte gets a warning ping and runs 15% faster for 3 s. At most once every 20 s. |

## Balance guard rails

- **Stuns:** the host can be dizzied by Shell Toss or Spin Move at most once
  every 15 s, so the two can't chain him.
- **Cheer!:** it recharges skills, not boosts, and never recharges another
  Vivienne, so two Viviennes can't loop it.
- **Shell Up:** it never blocks a doorway or cage door, because the host walks
  through her.
- **Never Give Up:** it only counts when she's the last kid still free, so it
  helps a comeback without making her fast all game.
