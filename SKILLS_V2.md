# Skills, version 2 - a proposal (8 Oct, for Brent to choose)

Nothing here is built yet. Pick, change or drop any line; then I build it in
the phases at the end.

## What is wrong with skills today

- **Half the team has no button.** Shadow (Sneak), Patch (Shield), Echo
  (Noise), Muscle (Barricade) and now Glow (Flare) press something; Tinker,
  Brainy and Bramble only walk up to things and press E. Their button just
  shows a tip. The new R key does nothing for them.
- **Most ladder picks are "+5% / +10%".** They add up, but nobody feels them.
- **Level 10 comes too soon** for keen players, then there is nothing to aim for.

## The plan in one line

Every character gets **a button of their own (level 1)**, a **big new power
to choose at level 10**, and a **capstone at level 20**. The other levels stay
small steps, but each character's own levels mostly *change how their skill
works* rather than adding a percent.

## Levels 1 to 20

- XP needed stays the same to level 10 (16,000), then grows more slowly:
  11: 20,000 · 12: 24,500 · 13: 29,500 · 14: 35,000 · 15: 41,000 ·
  16: 47,500 · 17: 54,500 · 18: 62,000 · 19: 70,000 · 20: 78,500.
  (About as much play again from 10 to 20 as from 1 to 10, twice over.)
- **Even levels (2-20): small team-wide steps**, one of two: a little more
  stamina / running / quiet steps / faster hands. From level 12 they are
  half the size (3% instead of 5-10%), and caps stop them stacking too far.
- **Odd levels (3-19): the character's own skill**, mostly new behaviour.
- **Level 10 and level 20: a choice of two big powers.**
- Nobody loses anything: levels and picks already made stay; XP already
  earned counts towards 11-20 straight away.

## Balance guard rails

- One big power each at 10 and 20, never both of a pair.
- Anything that stops the host (Ground Pound, Snapper, Sound Wave, Dazzle) is
  capped at 2 s and has a long recharge (45-60 s); the host can't be stunned
  twice within 8 s by anyone.
- Nightmare: the host shrugs off stuns after 1 s.
- The 📊 Balance table (after a week live) tells us if any character wins
  too often; numbers live in Config so they can be tuned without new code.

## Build in phases (my recommendation)

1. **Buttons for everyone** - Tinker's Toolkit, Brainy's Think!, Bramble's
   Vines, Echo's burst. Every character then uses R. (One build.)
2. **Levels 11-20** - the XP curve, half-size even levels, the odd levels.
3. **The level-10 and level-20 powers** - one character at a time, Shadow's
   Clone first.

Decisions for Brent: which of the table to keep or change; whether Echo's
burst is speed (my pick: you feel it more) or stamina; and the phase order.
