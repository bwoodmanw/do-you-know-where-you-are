# Side toon pictures - every other character, skin and host (11 Oct 2026)

Echo's format (`ECHO_SIDE_TOON_PICTURE_WORKFLOW.md`), for the other 33
models: 8 characters' and skins' left / right side pictures, the hosts, the
Gobbler and the turkey. `tools/paint_toon.py` paints the sides of each model
from these (Stable Fast 3D only guesses the sides).

The last 4 rows remake **Starlight Echo** with her headphones ON her ears
(her neck headphones melted into her jaw, as Echo's did). They save into a
new folder, `starlight-echo-remake`, so the approved pictures stay until
Claude checks the new ones. Delete those 4 rows if you'd rather keep her as
she is.

"Left" and "right" are always the character's OWN left and right. In a
front picture the character's own left is on the picture's right.

```
create new workflow to create images based on the [Picture Standard], [Reference] and [Picture]. For each picture, attach every [Reference] picture listed, in that order, then save to "C:\Users\bwood\OneDrive\Documents\Kids Games\Do You Know Where You Are\art\model-input\[file name]". For example, C:\Users\bwood\OneDrive\Documents\Kids Games\Do You Know Where You Are\art\model-input\brainy\left-toon.png. Create the folder if it does not exist. Do the rows in order: the last rows use pictures made by the rows before them.
```
