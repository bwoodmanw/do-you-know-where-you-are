# Brainy's skill ladder - approved and built (8 Oct)

Brent approved this as written; built 8 Oct (TEST_PLAN.md round 13). Mastermind:
the TEAM's first clue (whoever presses Clue first) is free and, instead of one
step, lists every step still to do: how to learn the code, each locked or
blocked door and how to get through it, then the keypad's room.

**Today** Brainy's own levels (3, 5, 7, 9, 10) are almost all "+N clues" or
"read faster", so he gets more of the same. Brainy's skill is already strong:
he reads the twisty invitation (the whole code, behind the first locked door)
and pays half for clues.

**Idea:** every level teaches Brainy something new about clues and the house,
instead of only more clues. One choice per level, as now. The even levels
(2, 4, 6, 8) stay the same for everyone.

| Lvl | Choice A | Choice B |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Bonus Hint** - +1 free clue for the team *(as now)* | **Trick Spotter** - trick balloons look grey and droopy to the whole team, so you never pop a fake *(new; replaces Code Sense)* |
| 5 | **Quick Reader** - read and use things 20% faster *(as now)* | **Two-Step Clue** - your clues also light up the step after next *(new; replaces Clever)* |
| 7 | **Notebook** - +1 free clue for the team *(as now)* | **Key Finder** - the room hiding each key shows a 🔑 on the team's corner map once anyone has been in it *(new; replaces Focus)* |
| 9 | **Speed Scan** - read and use things 20% faster *(as now)* | **Long Read** - read the invitation from twice as far away, even from the doorway *(new; replaces Big Brain +2)* |
| 10 | **Genius** - +3 free clues for the team *(as now)* | **Mastermind** - the first clue of every game is free and names every step to the exit (rooms, doors, keypad) *(new; replaces Speed Reader)* |

What it changes:
- Brainy no longer gets "one code colour" (his invitation already gives the
  whole code); the team can still get one colour from Tinker, Glow or Bramble.
- Clue numbers stay within the 5-free-clue cap.
- **Players who already chose a Brainy upgrade that is replaced** (Code Sense,
  Clever, Focus, Big Brain, Speed Reader) get a free re-pick at that level:
  the old choice simply stops counting and the level shows both new choices.

Build size: medium (the new upgrades need new game code: grey trick balloons,
a second highlight, map keys, a longer read, a full-route clue). About one
session's piece of work, tested in Studio.
