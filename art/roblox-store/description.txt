HALLOWEEN UPDATE: the Abandoned Hospital, Candy Factory, Jelly Vault, Muscle's Barricade and secret Store Rooms!

Can your crew escape before the host catches you? Team up with up to 6 friends or go solo, pick a hero with a special skill, solve the colour code and get out!

HOW TO PLAY
- Pop clue balloons to learn the door code (some are tricks!)
- Search drawers and boxes for keys and surprises
- Run, hide and crawl through vents when the host comes
- Free caught friends from the cage
- Escape a floor to unlock the next one

8 HEROES: Tinker picks locks, Shadow sneaks, Brainy solves codes, Muscle builds barricades, Glow lights up secrets, Patch shields friends, Echo throws noises, Bramble talks to plants

3 buildings, 9 floors, 3 hosts: the Pumpkin, the Gummy Bear Man and the Robot

Level up your heroes, claim daily rewards and top the leaderboards. Giggly or Spooky - you choose!
