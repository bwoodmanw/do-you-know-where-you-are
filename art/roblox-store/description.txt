🎃 HALLOWEEN UPDATE: the Abandoned Hospital, Candy Factory, Jelly Vault, Muscle's Barricade and Store Rooms!

Can your crew escape before the host catches you? Team up with up to 6 friends or go solo, pick a hero with a special skill, crack the colour code and get OUT!

HOW TO PLAY
- Pop clue balloons for the door code (some are tricks!)
- Search drawers and boxes for keys and Party Poppers
- Run, hide and crawl through vents when the host comes
- Free caught friends from the cage
- Escape a floor to unlock the next one

8 HEROES: Tinker picks locks, Shadow sneaks, Brainy cracks codes, Muscle barricades, Glow lights up secrets, Patch shields friends, Echo throws noises, Bramble talks to plants

3 buildings, 9 floors, 3 hosts: the Pumpkin, the Gummy Bear Man and the Robot

Level heroes to 10, claim daily rewards and top the leaderboards. Giggly or Spooky - you choose!
