CHRISTMAS UPDATE: Claus Manor, Candy Cane Lane and Elfy, the limited-edition elf!

Can your crew escape before the host catches you? Team up with up to 6 friends or go solo, pick a hero with a special skill, crack the color code and get out!

CHRISTMAS (December 1 - 31)
- Claus Manor: open presents while grumpy Mr and Mrs Claus hunt you - watch out for decoys!
- Open 150 presents to win Elfy, the limited-edition elf
- Candy Cane Lane: sneak past Crumbles the Gingerbread Giant
- Find candy canes on every floor and spend them on 13 Christmas skins
- Nutmeg the reindeer kid joins the crew

HOW TO PLAY
- Pop clue balloons to learn the door code
- Search drawers and boxes for keys
- Run, hide and crawl through vents when the host comes
- Free caught friends from the cage
- Escape a floor to unlock the next one

14 HEROES, each with a special skill and upgrades
6 buildings and 6 hosts, plus holiday buildings

Level up your heroes, claim daily rewards and top the leaderboards!
