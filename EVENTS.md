# Roblox Events pages (Creator Hub -> Events)

An Event page shows on Escape Crew's game page with a **Notify me** button,
so players get a reminder when it starts. Each event needs a 16:9 picture
(made with `tools/make_event_art.py`) and the text below. No emoji in the
text (like the store description), American English.

## 1. Halloween - Candy Corn Hunt (running now, until November 1)

Picture: `art/roblox-store/events/event-halloween.png` (1920 x 1080).

- **Title:** Candy Corn Hunt
- **Subtitle:** Find candy corns on every floor
- **Description:**
  Halloween has come to every floor of Escape Crew! Six glowing candy corns
  are hidden on every floor of every building. Grab them while you escape,
  then spend them in the Lobby's Candy Shop on boosts - or save 100 for the
  Muscle Mummy skin. Find 50 for the Candy Corn Collector badge. The hunt
  ends November 1, so get your crew together!
- **Starts:** now (today)
- **Ends:** November 1, 12:00 AM Eastern Time (October 31, 9:00 PM Pacific) -
  the moment the corns disappear from the game.
- **Category:** Quest (a goal to complete for rewards: collect corns, earn the
  badge and the skin). Not Item Drop (that means avatar items to get).
- **Start Place:** Escape Crew. **Privacy:** Public.
- **Submit for Featuring:** on (free; Roblox may pick it for its own
  marketing, nothing to lose).

**Live since 9 Oct.**

## Click by click (Creator Hub; menus move between versions - send a photo if a screen differs)

1. Open **create.roblox.com** and sign in as Bwoodmanw.
2. Click **Creations**, then the **Escape Crew** experience.
3. In the left menu, open **Engagement** and click **Events**
   (some versions call it **Events and Updates**).
4. Click **Create Event**.
5. Fill in **Title** and **Description** from above (copy and paste).
6. Set **Start** to now and **End** to the date and time above.
7. Under the picture / thumbnail section click **Upload** and pick
   `art/roblox-store/events/event-halloween.png`.
8. If it asks which place the event is in, pick **Escape Crew** (the
   Lobby, the start place).
9. Pick the category, then click **Publish** (or **Save**, then **Publish**).
10. Open the game's page on roblox.com: the event should show under the
    description with **Notify me**. Send Claude a photo.
