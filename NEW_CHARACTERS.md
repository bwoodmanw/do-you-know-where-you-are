# Escape Crew - four new characters: ideas (3 Nov 2026)

Brent asked for themes, skills and looks for **Harmony, Clone, Cloud** and a
**Reindeer** (Christmas). Nothing is built yet - this is for choosing.

## What the eight already cover (so the new ones don't overlap)

| Character | Job in a floor | Button skill |
|---|---|---|
| Tinker | picks locked doors / a keypad color | Toolkit: instant pick |
| Shadow | hard to spot | Sneak: unseen and ungrabbable |
| Brainy | reads the twisty invitation (door code) | Think!: lights the next step |
| Muscle | shoves junk out of doorways | Barricade: crate in a doorway |
| Glow | lights dark rooms, secret paint (code) | Flare: far light, stamina, speed |
| Patch | stamina for friends, frees the cage | Shield |
| Echo | hears the host further away | Noise: sends the host elsewhere |
| Bramble | the big plant whispers the code | Vines: slows the host in a doorway |

Gaps nobody fills yet: **decoys**, **height** (high shelves, jumping gaps),
**hiding the whole team at once**, **finding keys from far away**, **moving
together as a team**, **music / sound puzzles**.

## Harmony - the team's heart (music)

- **Look:** a girl with big headphones around her neck, a lavender
  hoodie with sound-wave stripes, a small keyboard-style wristband, sneakers
  in two colors. (Echo is the DJ/noise kid - Harmony is melody, softer
  colors.)
- **Floor job: the music box.** A music box in a room open from the start
  plays the door code as three notes; Harmony turns each note into its color
  (a 4th way to learn the code, beside Brainy, Glow and Bramble).
- **Passive: In Tune.** Friends within a few steps of Harmony get their
  stamina back faster (Patch gives a lump of stamina; Harmony is a steady
  trickle - reward for sticking together).
- **Button: Lullaby.** For 6 seconds the host near Harmony slows down and
  can't hear running. Solo-friendly.
- **Level 10 power: Chorus** - every friend's skill recharges a little.
  **Level 20: Encore** - Lullaby reaches the whole floor once a game.

## Clone - the decoy kid (gadgets)

- **Look:** a boy with a two-tone jacket (left half teal, right half
  orange), a chunky wrist gadget with a screen, goggles pushed up. Not
  armour, not a helmet - nothing like a famous sci-fi soldier.
- **Floor job: copier.** The office copier prints a second copy of a key
  - one key opens a door for two kids at once, so the team can split up.
- **Button: Copy.** Drops a glowing copy of Clone that runs off in the
  direction Clone faces; the host chases it for up to 5 seconds, then it
  pops into sparkles.
- **Level 10 power: Swap** - tap again while the copy is out to swap places
  with it (the great escape move).
  **Level 20: Double Copy** - two copies, running different ways.

## Cloud - the floaty one (weather)

- **Look:** a kid with fluffy white-and-sky-blue hair, a yellow raincoat
  with a cloud pattern, a little cloud-shaped backpack, rain boots. Soft
  and round - and nothing like the famous spiky-haired game hero called
  Cloud (no spiky blond hair, no giant sword).
- **Floor job: high places.** Cloud floats up to the top shelf or the attic
  hatch, where a key or a present waits that nobody else can reach.
- **Passive: Light Step.** Falls slowly and jumps a little higher.
- **Button: Fog.** Breathes out a fog cloud for 6 seconds: anyone standing
  in it is hidden from the host's eyes (Shadow hides one kid; Fog hides the
  team, but stays in one place).
- **Level 10 power: Drift** - a short glide across a room.
  **Level 20: Storm Cloud** - the fog also makes the host sneeze and stop.

## The Reindeer (Christmas) - working name "Sprout" or "Blitz"

- **Look:** a kid in a cozy reindeer hoodie - fuzzy antlers on the hood, a
  green scarf, mittens, a small brass bell on the scarf. **No glowing red
  nose** (that's a famous trademarked reindeer). A name of our own, not one
  of Santa's eight.
- **Floor job: present sniffer.** Presents and keys within range glow
  through walls for Reindeer for a moment when it sniffs.
- **Button: Sleigh Dash.** A fast dash for 2 seconds that can carry one
  friend along (both zoom) - the team-escape button.
- **Level 10 power: Snow Sense** - Sniff also shows the nearest key's
  direction with an arrow.
  **Level 20: Jingle Bells** - the bell rings and the host freezes for
  3 seconds, once a game.
- **When:** the Christmas character. Unlocked during the Christmas event
  (earned with the Christmas item, like the season skins), then playable
  for good by those who earned it.

## What each one takes to make

For each: the pictures (front, side, back) in Brent's ChatGPT workflow, the
model (Stable Fast 3D, paint_toon, the checker), Studio import and Avatar
Setup, a portrait, the skill (server + HUD), floor-job props on every floor,
a 20-level skill ladder, and room in the character pickers (8 -> 12).
Roughly one character every few days of work. The Reindeer is the only one
with a deadline (the Christmas event).
