# Escape Crew - four new characters (3 Nov 2026, second round)

Brent's answers: **Reindeer first**; Harmony and Cloud are girls, Clone a
boy; the Reindeer is bought (more than a skin) or earned with a high count
of the Christmas item. Nothing is built yet.

## The words used below

- **Always on** - works by itself, nothing to press (Echo hears the host
  from further away).
- **Floor job** - something only this character can do at a certain thing on
  the floor: walk up and hold E (Brainy reads the invitation, Bramble the
  plant). Shadow, Echo and Patch have none - it's optional.
- **Button** - press it; it works for a few seconds, then recharges.
- **Big powers** - level 10 and level 20 picks on the skill ladder. No extra
  button: once picked, they change the button or the always-on part.

Game numbers for scale (Normal): kids walk 12 and run 20 studs a second, the
host hunts at 14.5; Shadow's Sneak lasts 6 seconds; keys and Confetti
Cannons hide in drawers and boxes; bonus presents are rewards.

## The Reindeer (Christmas) - name to pick

- **Look:** a kid in a cozy reindeer hoodie - fuzzy antlers on the hood, a
  green scarf, mittens, a small brass bell. No glowing red nose, no name of
  Santa's eight.
- **Names (Sprout is taken):** Juniper, Tinsel, Nutmeg, Pinecone, Flurry,
  Clove. (Blitz is too close to Blitzen.) Check each against games Brent
  knows before choosing.
- **Always on - Good Nose:** drawers and boxes within about 20 studs that
  hold a key or a Confetti Cannon sparkle through walls - only on the
  Reindeer's screen. (Not bonus presents: those already show.)
- **Button - Sleigh Dash:** about 1.5 seconds at double running speed
  (roughly 40 studs, two rooms). The nearest friend within about 12 studs (a
  few steps) is pulled along beside the Reindeer automatically - no aiming.
  Walls stop the dash.
- **Level 10 - Snow Sense:** after a dash, an arrow points to the nearest
  key for 5 seconds.
- **Level 20 - Jingle Bells:** once a game, the bell rings and the host
  freezes for **5 seconds**.
- **How to get it:** a Game Pass (recommended **199 Robux**; skins are 99)
  or **300** of the Christmas item (a skin is 150). Earned or bought, it's
  yours for good.

## Clone - a boy, the decoy

- **Look:** a two-tone jacket (left half teal, right half orange), a chunky
  wrist gadget, goggles pushed up. No armour or helmet.
- **What changed:** the key copier is gone - every locked door has its own
  key, so a copied key couldn't open a second door. **Swap is gone** -
  Brent is right: the copy is where the host is chasing, so swapping puts
  Clone in danger.
- **How it differs:** Shadow hides only himself. Echo's noise sends the host
  to *look* somewhere, before it has found anyone. Clone's copy **breaks a
  chase that's already happening, for any friend**.
- **Always on - Look-alike:** the host hesitates for a second before
  grabbing Clone (it thinks he might be a copy).
- **Button - Copy:** a glowing copy of Clone appears beside him and runs the
  other way. A host chasing Clone or any friend within about 20 studs
  switches to the copy for 5 seconds; then the copy pops into sparkles.
- **Level 10 - Pop!:** when the host catches the copy, it bursts into
  confetti and the host is dazed for 2 seconds.
- **Level 20 - Double Copy:** one press makes two copies running different
  ways; the copies last 7 seconds. (Not about doors - it's just more
  confusion for the host.)

## Cloud - a girl, the fog

- **Look:** almost-white hair (a soft bob with a faint blue tint), a yellow
  raincoat with a cloud pattern, a little cloud backpack, rain boots. Nothing
  like the spiky-haired game hero.
- **What changed:** Light Step and Drift are gone - our floors are houses
  with few gaps or drops, so they would hardly help.
- **Floor job - Float Up:** Cloud floats up to high spots marked with a
  small cloud - a top shelf or an attic hatch. These must be built on every
  floor (two or three each). They hold **extras only** - a bonus present or
  a free clue, never a key the team needs, so a party without Cloud is never
  stuck.
- **Button - Fog:** a fog cloud about 10 studs across (a hallway or a corner
  of a room) for **8 seconds**. Anyone inside is hidden. A host that was
  chasing them loses track and searches the last place it saw them, like
  when a kid hides in a closet. Fog doesn't push the host away: it breaks
  the chase, and the kids slip out the far side while the host searches.
- **Level 10 - Rolling Fog:** the fog drifts along with Cloud at walking
  speed, so the team can move while hidden.
- **Level 20 - Storm Cloud:** the same fog becomes a storm: it still hides
  kids, and a host that walks into it sneezes and stops for 3 seconds (once
  per fog).
- Shadow vs Fog: different characters, each with their own button from
  level 1 - Shadow's Sneak hides Shadow alone and moves with him; Fog hides
  everyone in it but stays put (until Rolling Fog).

## Harmony - a girl, music

- **Look:** big headphones around her neck, a lavender hoodie with
  sound-wave stripes, a keyboard-style wristband.
- **Floor job - Music Box:** a music box in a room open from the start plays
  the door code as three notes; Harmony turns them into the colors (a 4th
  way to the code, beside Brainy, Glow and Bramble). Built on every floor.
- **Always on - In Tune:** friends within a few steps slowly get stamina
  back.
- **Button - Lullaby:** for 6 seconds the host near Harmony walks at half
  speed.
- **Level 10 - Chorus:** Lullaby also takes 5 seconds off every friend's
  skill recharge. **Level 20 - Encore:** once a game, Lullaby reaches the
  whole floor.
