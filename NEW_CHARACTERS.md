# Escape Crew - new characters: the build spec (3 Nov 2026, decided)

Brent's decisions, 3 Nov: **Nutmeg** (the Christmas reindeer, a girl) is
built first, then **Cloud** (a girl) and **Clone** (a boy); **Harmony** (a
girl) later. The Christmas item is **candy canes**. Nothing is built yet.

## The words used below

- **Always on** - works by itself, nothing to press.
- **Floor job** - something only this character can do at a certain thing on
  the floor: walk up and hold E (Brainy's invitation, Bramble's plant).
  Optional - Shadow, Echo and Patch have none.
- **Button** - press it; it works for a few seconds, then recharges.
- **Skill ladder** - like the eight today: levels 2-20, two choices at each
  level. Even levels are the same for everyone (stamina, speed, hands,
  quiet). Odd levels, 10 and 20 are the character's own, below. Levels 10 and
  20 are the **big powers**: no extra button, they change the button or the
  always-on part (Clone's Copy Two is the one exception - see Clone).

Game numbers for scale (Normal): kids walk 12 and run 20 studs a second, the
host hunts at 14.5. Today's recharge times: Shadow 20 s, Echo 25, Muscle 25,
Glow 30, Bramble 30, Tinker 40, Patch 45, Brainy 60.

## Nutmeg - the Christmas reindeer (a girl)

- **Look:** a girl in a cozy reindeer hoodie, hood up with short chunky
  antlers and small ears, a green scarf with a brass bell, red mittens. No
  red nose; not one of Santa's eight names (Sprout is taken in another
  game).
- **How to get her:** a **399 Robux** Game Pass, or **400 candy canes** in
  the Christmas shop. Earned or bought, she's yours for good. (A skin is 99
  Robux or 150 of the season's item.)
- **Always on - Good Nose:** drawers and boxes within 20 studs that hold a
  key or a Confetti Cannon sparkle through walls, on Nutmeg's screen only.
- **Button - Sleigh Dash (recharge 25 s):** about 1.5 s at double running
  speed (roughly 40 studs). The nearest friend within 12 studs is pulled
  along beside her - no aiming. Walls stop the dash.

## Cloud - the fog (a girl)

- **Look:** almost-white rounded bob with a faint blue tint, yellow raincoat,
  puffy cloud backpack, sky-blue rain boots. Nothing like the spiky-haired
  game hero.
- **Floor job - Float Up:** she floats up to high spots marked with a small
  cloud (a top shelf, an attic hatch), two or three built on every floor.
  They hold extras only - a bonus present or a free clue, never a key the
  team needs.
- **Button - Fog (recharge 35 s):** a fog cloud about 10 studs across for
  8 s. Anyone inside is hidden; a host chasing them loses track and searches
  the last place it saw them. The kids slip out the far side.

## Clone - the copycat (a boy)

- **Look:** two-tone jacket (left half teal, right half orange), a chunky
  wrist gadget, goggles pushed up. No armour or helmet.
- **Why Copycat:** Shadow's ladder already has the decoy (Shadow Clone,
  Lasting Shadow, Double Clone), so Clone borrows his friends' skills
  instead.
- **Always on - Look-alike:** the host hesitates 1 s before grabbing Clone.
- **Button - Copycat (recharge 90 s - the longest, as it brings another
  button):** copies the button skill of the nearest friend (within 20
  studs). That skill appears as a **second button** beside Copycat, works
  at its basic strength and **recharges at the friend's own rate** (Glow's
  Flare every 30 s, Shadow's Sneak every 20 s...). Clone keeps it until he
  presses Copycat again.
- Only buttons are copied, never floor jobs - with one bonus: a copied
  Toolkit lets Clone pick the next locked door himself.
- **Alone** (Solo, or nobody within 20 studs): a spinning wheel picks one of
  the other characters' buttons for him.

## Harmony - music (a girl, later)

- **Look:** music-note hair clips and a small music-box pendant (no
  headphones - Echo wears those), a lavender hoodie with sound-wave stripes,
  a keyboard-style wristband.
- **Floor job - Music Box:** a music box plays the door code as three notes;
  Harmony turns them into the colors. Built on every floor.
- **Always on - In Tune:** friends within a few steps slowly get stamina back.
- **Button - Lullaby:** for 6 s the host near Harmony walks at half speed.
- **Level 10 - Chorus:** Lullaby also takes 5 s off every friend's recharge.
  **Level 20 - Encore:** once a game, Lullaby reaches the whole floor.
  (Full ladder when she is next.)
